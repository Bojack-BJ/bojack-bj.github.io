Hi, I am Xiaotong Li. I am currently completing my MSc in Robotics at Delft University of Technology in the Netherlands. Before that, I received my BEng in Aircraft Design and Engineering from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics.

My current research interests include world models, open-world manipulation, robot planning, and embodied autonomy. I am particularly interested in systems that can reason about object dynamics and use simulation or structured scene understanding before acting in the real world.

I am seeking PhD opportunities in robotics. My CV is available [here](/uploads/cv.pdf).
